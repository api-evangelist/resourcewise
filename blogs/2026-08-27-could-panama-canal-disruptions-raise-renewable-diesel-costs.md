Low rainfall across the Panama Canal watershed has prompted the Panama Canal Authority to reduce the number of vessels allowed through the waterway beginning in September. For renewable diesel producers on the US Gulf Coast, those restrictions could complicate one of the key routes used to move product to California and other West Coast markets and potentially increase cost.
