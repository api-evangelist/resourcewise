The Internal Revenue Service recently issued Notice 2026-53, providing the 2026 emissions rate table for the Section 45Z Clean Fuel Production Credit and guidance on agricultural practices, manure-derived fuels, and the application of earlier legislative changes. The notice advances implementation while the broader proposed 45Z regulations remain under final consideration.
