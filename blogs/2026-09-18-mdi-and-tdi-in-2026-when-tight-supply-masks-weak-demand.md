MDI and TDI markets have been shaped by an unusual combination of forces in 2026. Demand recovery has remained uneven, with several important polyurethane end-use sectors continuing to face subdued conditions. At the same time, supply disruptions, geopolitical conflict, volatile feedstocks costs and changing trade flows have repeatedly altered market conditions.
