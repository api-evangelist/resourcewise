Biofuel blending is the process of combining renewable, organically-derived fuels such as ethanol or biodiesel with conventional petroleum products such as gasoline or diesel. The resulting blend can reduce dependence on fossil fuels, support regulatory compliance, and lower the carbon intensity of transportation fuels.
